Why some numbers are still blank

Each forecast is saved when it's made. As demand measurements arrive, we check those hours and update the errors above. A new forecast may have no results to check yet.

Choose an older forecast to see a fuller comparison. These results describe that one day's forecast.

About these results

Overall error (WAPE) adds up the absolute hourly misses and divides by total measured demand. Average hourly error (MAE) is the mean absolute miss. Both use only hours with measurements.

Weather is based on Charlotte, one location within the service area. The model is an experiment, and performance can vary by season.
